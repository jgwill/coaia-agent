## Minimum Field Expectations and Relevance
1. **Requirements engineering / requirements traceability**  
   Ensures every artifact and decision can be traced to issue/rispec/chart handles and verified later.
2. **HCI and CSCW**  
   Grounds interface and collaboration behavior across user, Atlas, and delegated agents.
3. **Knowledge organization and information science**  
   Supports durable indexing, retrieval, and packet reuse across sessions and repositories.
4. **Archival studies, provenance, and records management**  
   Preserves source lineage and trustworthy change history for narrative capability evolution.
5. **Narratology, narrative theory, and transmedia storytelling**  
   Connects continuity, structure, and cross-medium narrative coherence in Chronicle outputs.
6. **Computational creativity and AI-assisted writing**  
   Frames how model-generated text can assist while preserving intent and revision discipline.
7. **Multi-agent systems and human-AI collaboration**  
   Clarifies delegation contracts, role boundaries, and handoff reliability.
8. **AI ethics, consent, and cultural safety**  
   Defines safety/consent boundaries, especially for sensitive reflective content.
9. **Indigenous / relational methodologies (careful, contextual use)**  
   Introduces relational accountability where relevant without flattening or appropriating context.
10. **Sociology of creative labor and creative practice**  
    Locates Chronicle-making in real creative workflows, constraints, and sustained practice.

## Baseline Rule
Name and justify fields **before** over-collecting sources. Coverage quality is measured against this map first.
